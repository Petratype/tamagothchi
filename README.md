Tamagothchi Ghost 👻

Meet your tiny, pixelated goth ghost! Take care of its hunger, happiness, and energy as it grows from a cute baby ghost to a spooky adult. Keep it alive, have fun feeding, playing, and letting it nap… and try not to let it fade away!

All the pixel art you see? Made by yours truly in Piskel. The eerie tunes in the background? Composed in BeepBox.

It’s silly, spooky, and retro — like a 90s ghost tamagotchi with a modern twist. Press START, watch it appear through the clouds, and enjoy your new tiny ghost buddy!
