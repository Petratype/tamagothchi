
Tamagothchi Ghost 👻

Meet this little tiny, pixelated goth ghost! Take care of its hunger, happiness and energy as it grows from a cute baby ghost to a spooky adult. Keep it alive, have fun feeding, playing, and letting it nap… and try not to let it fade away!

All the pixel art you see? Made in Piskel. The funny 8-bit inspired tunes in the background? Composed in BeepBox. Sound effects and winning song came from Freesound.

It’s silly, spooky and retro, like a 90s ghost tamagotchi with a modern twist. Inspired in my childhood and personal interests. Looking forward to make it better! 

Press START and enjoy this little first version and play with your new tiny ghost buddy!
